about

Andrew Acciaioli (he/him) is a creative nonfiction filmmaker and photographer based in metro Detroit, Michigan. He holds a BA in Film & Media Production from Michigan State University, with minors in Documentary Filmmaking and Music. Andrew seeks to explore the convergence between the built and natural worlds through experimental modes of cinema vérité and personal filmmaking, as well as explorations of non-conventional camera, lens, and projection technologies.
